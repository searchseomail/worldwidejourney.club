Don't let the gear intimidate you! For a budget setup, grab a Samson Q2U or an Audio-Technica ATR2100x. Both are USB/XLR dynamic mics, meaning you can start via USB and upgrade to an audio interface later. They isolate background noise way better than cheap condenser mics.
