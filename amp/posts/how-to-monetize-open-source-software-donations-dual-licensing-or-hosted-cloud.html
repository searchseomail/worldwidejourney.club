How to Monetize Open-Source Software: Donations, Dual-Licensing, or Hosted Cloud?

RogueRebel OP
05.01.2025 14:44
I've been maintaining a growing open-source project for over a year now, and the community adoption has been incredible. However, the time commitment is starting to rival my full-time job, and I need to figure out a sustainable way to monetize it without alienating my current user base. I am torn between relying on community donations, setting up a dual-licensing model, or offering a fully managed hosted cloud version. What strategies have worked best for you all in practice?

Hosted cloud (SaaS) is definitely the way to go if your software lends itself to being run as a service. Enterprise users don't want to mess with self-hosting if they can just pay $50 a month to have someone else handle the ops, backups, and scaling.

Dual-licensing can work great, but it really depends on your target audience. If you are selling to large enterprises with strict compliance needs, offering a commercial license alongside the AGPL or GPL is a proven path. Just make sure your contributors sign a CLA so you actually own the copyright.
