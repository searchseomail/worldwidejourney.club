HYSAs offer great liquidity, but I'm worried about fluctuating interest rates dropping over the next year. On the other hand, locking money into a 12-month CD gives me a guaranteed rate, but the early withdrawal penalties make me nervous if an unexpected expense pops up. Where are you currently keeping your emergency cash, and how do you weigh the flexibility of an HYSA against the guaranteed returns of a CD?
