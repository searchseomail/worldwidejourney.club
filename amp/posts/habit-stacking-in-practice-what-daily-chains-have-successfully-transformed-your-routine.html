Which cues and behaviors did you link together that genuinely stuck without feeling like a chore?

If your anchor habit is collapsing, you are probably picking an anchor that happens at a variable time. Try tying your chain to a physiological need instead of a task, like the exact moment your feet hit the floor in the morning, followed immediately by drinking a full glass of water.

I struggled with this too until I stopped trying to reinvent my whole routine at once. My morning chain is: alarm goes off -> phone goes across the room -> immediate 5-minute stretch. If the anchor isn't reliable, try setting a specific phone alarm to act as an artificial cue until the habit is hardwired.

A huge game-changer for me was reducing friction by preparing my environment the night before. If I want to read before bed, I put the book directly on my pillow right after making the bed in the morning. When my head hits the pillow at night, the book is right there.

Keep your first links ridiculously small. Like, two minutes max. If your chain requires thirty minutes of sustained willpower right out of the gate, it's going to collapse. Start with 'after I pour my water, I will meditate for 60 seconds.'

I used to fail at habit stacking because I tried to chain five or six things together immediately. Scale it back to a strict two-step chain: Anchor -> Behavior. Once that pair is automatic for a couple of weeks, then and only then should you add a third link to the chain.
