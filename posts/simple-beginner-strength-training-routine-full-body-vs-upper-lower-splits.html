Simple Beginner Strength Training Routine: Full Body vs. Upper-Lower Splits?

JungleJester
Topic Starter
Posted: 05.01.2025 08:35
I'm relatively new to lifting weights and trying to set up a consistent schedule that I can actually stick to long-term. I keep seeing conflicting advice on whether a full-body routine three times a week is better than a traditional upper-lower split for a complete beginner. Since I only have about 3 to 4 days available to train each week, which approach would yield the best muscle and strength gains without causing burnout? I'd love to hear what has worked best for those of you who have already gone through this initial stage.

With 3 to 4 days available, a 3-day full-body routine is honestly the absolute best starting point for a beginner. It hits every major muscle group multiple times a week without requiring marathon gym sessions.

If you can only commit to 3 days, full-body wins hands down. Upper-lower splits usually shine when you have 4 dedicated days to train, otherwise your frequency per muscle group drops too low.

Go with full-body. As a beginner, your central nervous system needs practice with the big lifts (squat, bench, deadlift/row) more than it needs high volume per body part.
