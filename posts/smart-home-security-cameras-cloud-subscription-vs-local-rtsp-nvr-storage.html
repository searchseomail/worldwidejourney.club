Smart Home Security Cameras: Cloud Subscription vs. Local RTSP/NVR Storage?

FrostbitePhantom
Topic Starter
Posted: 05.02.2025 04:08
I am currently overhauling my home security setup and torn between sticking to mainstream cloud-based cameras like Ring/Arlo or moving towards a local RTSP setup integrated with Home Assistant or an NVR. While the cloud subscription offers convenience and easy remote access, the recurring costs and privacy implications are starting to bother me. On the other hand, local storage seems more secure and cost-effective in the long run, but I am worried about the initial setup complexity and reliability. What are your experiences and recommendations for a future-proof system?

One thing to watch out for with local RTSP setups is disk write wear. Make sure you use surveillance-rated hard drives like WD Purple, otherwise standard desktop drives will fail prematurely from constant 24/7 writing.
