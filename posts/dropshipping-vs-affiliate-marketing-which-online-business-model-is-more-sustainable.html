Dropshipping vs. Affiliate Marketing: Which Online Business Model Is More Sustainable?

RogueRebel
Topic Starter
Posted: 11.03.2025 12:24
I'm currently trying to decide between starting a dropshipping store or diving into affiliate marketing for my next online venture. Both seem to have great potential, but I'm really concerned about long-term sustainability, profit margins, and the amount of upfront capital required. For those of you who have experience with either or both models, which one do you think is better suited for a beginner looking to build a stable income over the next few years?
