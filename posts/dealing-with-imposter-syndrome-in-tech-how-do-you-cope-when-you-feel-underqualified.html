Dealing with Imposter Syndrome in Tech: How Do You Cope When You Feel Underqualified?

ZenithZephyr
Topic Starter
Posted: 08.01.2025 09:39
I've been working as a software engineer for over two years now, but every time I start a new sprint or look at senior codebases, I feel like a total fraud who doesn't belong here. Even though I pass code reviews and deliver features, the nagging fear that everyone will realize I have no idea what I'm doing is exhausting. How do you all cope with imposter syndrome in the tech industry without burning out?

If it makes you feel any better, I've been doing this for over a decade and I still google basic syntax sometimes. Senior codebases look terrifying because they usually are terrifying.

Focus on the fact that you are passing code reviews and delivering features. Data doesn't lie—your team trusts your output, even if your brain is telling you otherwise.
EchoEmber

When my back starts hurting from staring at code all day stressing over this, I try to adjust my setup. Honestly, switching to ergonomic standing desks helped my physical posture, which weirdly helped my mental fatigue a bit too.
HyperionHero
Replied: 23.04.2025 08:42
Keep a 'wins' folder. Whenever someone compliments your PR or you fix a nasty bug, screenshot it and put it in there. Read it on days when the imposter syndrome hits hardest.
ObsidianOwl

CosmicCoder
