High-Yield Savings Accounts (HYSA) vs. CDs: Where Do You Keep Your Emergency Fund?

BlazeBrawler
Topic Starter
Posted: 24.01.2025 12:48
I'm currently restructuring my emergency fund and trying to decide whether to stick with a traditional high-yield savings account or lock some of the cash into a certificate of deposit. HYSAs offer great liquidity, but I'm worried about fluctuating interest rates dropping over the next year. On the other hand, locking money into a 12-month CD gives me a guaranteed rate, but the early withdrawal penalties make me nervous if an unexpected expense pops up. Where are you currently keeping your emergency cash, and how do you weigh the flexibility of an HYSA against the guaranteed returns of a CD?
