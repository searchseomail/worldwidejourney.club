Best Smartwatch for Android Users: Galaxy Watch, Pixel Watch, or Garmin?

FusionFalcon
Topic Starter

I'm currently using a standard Android phone and looking to upgrade my daily wearable, but I am completely torn between the latest Galaxy Watch, the Google Pixel Watch, and a Garmin model. I mainly care about accurate fitness tracking, good battery life, and smooth notification management without needing to charge the device every single night. Which of these ecosystems offers the best overall experience for a non-Samsung Android user?
