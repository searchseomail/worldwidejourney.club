Best Note-Taking App for Students and Professionals: Obsidian, Notion, or Apple Notes?

WarpedWanderer
Topic Starter
Posted: 11.01.2025 09:20
I am trying to revamp my productivity system and can't decide which note-taking app to commit to for the long term. I need something that works well for both academic studying and professional project management without feeling overly complicated. Between the markdown linking of Obsidian, the database power of Notion, and the sheer speed and simplicity of Apple Notes, which one should I choose?

Replies (8)

XenoXplorer
Replied: 15.01.2025 09:17
Honestly, I started with Notion because of the databases, but it got way too slow for quick daily notes. I switched to Apple Notes for speed, but if you want long-term knowledge management without lock-in, Obsidian is unbeatable.
CyberCenturion
Replied: 24.02.2025 09:05
If you are doing any kind of heavy studying, Obsidian's backlinking is a game changer. It forces you to connect ideas rather than just piling them up. Plus, your files are just local markdown documents, so you actually own your data.
FusionFalcon
Replied: 15.03.2025 08:45
I tried to use Notion for professional project management, but the maintenance overhead is unreal. I spend more time tweaking templates than actually working. Sometimes simpler is better, especially when you are also trying to learn how to break into the tech industry without burning out.
VortexVagabond
Replied: 08.05.2025 04:07
Apple Notes has quietly gotten so good with the latest updates. Tags, quick notes, and proper PDF markup make it more than enough for 90% of people. Don't overcomplicate your system unless you truly need relational databases.
ShadowStalker
Replied: 22.05.2025 14:35
Notion is the king of project management if you work on a team, but for personal academic use, it's overkill. I use Notion for work and Apple Notes for everything else.
ChromeChaser
Replied: 17.07.2025 00:20
The biggest trap is spending weeks setting up the 'perfect' productivity system. Pick Obsidian if you love graph views and markdown, or Apple Notes if you just want to open an app and start typing immediately without friction.
CrypticCorsair
Replied: 10.08.2025 10:21
When I was first figuring out how to manage client projects and track notes, Notion saved my life. But as my business grew, the loading times drove me crazy. Obsidian is much faster once you learn the shortcuts.

Just go with Apple Notes. You can always export later if you outgrow it. Most people spend more time organizing notes than actually studying or working.
