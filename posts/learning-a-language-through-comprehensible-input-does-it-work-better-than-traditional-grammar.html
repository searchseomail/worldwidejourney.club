Learning a Language Through Comprehensible Input: Does It Work Better Than Traditional Grammar?

ShadowStalker
Topic Starter
Posted: 12.01.2025 01:54
I keep seeing influencers and polyglots online claiming that memorizing grammar rules and doing textbook exercises is a waste of time compared to just consuming massive amounts of native content. Lately, I've been experimenting with the Comprehensible Input method for Spanish, and while it feels much more natural, I sometimes worry I'm missing out on structural foundations. For those of you who have tried both approaches seriously, does pure CI really outperform traditional grammar study in the long run?
