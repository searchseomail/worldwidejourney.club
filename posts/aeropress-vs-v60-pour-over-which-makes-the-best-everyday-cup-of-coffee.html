Topic Starter

I'm currently trying to decide between picking up an AeroPress or a Hario V60 for my daily morning routine. I love a clean cup with bright tasting notes, but I also value convenience and speed when I'm half-asleep before work. For those of you who have used both extensively, which one wins out for an everyday brewer in terms of consistency, ease of cleaning, and overall flavor profile?
