Lubing Mechanical Keyboard Switches: Is the Time Investment Truly Noticeable?

YieldingYeti
Topic Starter
Posted: 22.01.2025 12:09
I've been staring at a tub of Krytox 205g0 and a brush for a week now, but the thought of painstakingly opening and hand-lubing 87 switches gives me serious procrastination. Is the actual difference in sound and smoothness genuinely night-and-day, or is it just an overhyped enthusiast rite of passage? I'm currently using stock Gateron Browns and just want to know if spending an entire Saturday on this project is actually worth the hours put in.
