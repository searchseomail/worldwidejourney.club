BinaryBard
Topic Starter
Posted: 09.02.2025 04:30
I finally took the plunge and set up Home Assistant on an old mini PC, and I've got my basic lights and thermostat connected. However, browsing through hundreds of blueprints and forum posts is honestly a bit overwhelming for a beginner. I want to avoid the trap of setting up 'smart' things just for the novelty factor and focus on automations that actually improve daily life. Which simple automations do you actually rely on every single day without fail?
