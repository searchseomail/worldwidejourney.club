Practical Minimalist Living: What Everyday Clutter Was Hardest for You to Give Up?

VelvetViper
Topic Starter
Posted: 09.02.2025 05:29
I've been on a minimalist journey for about six months now, and while clearing out the obvious junk felt great, I've hit a serious wall with sentimental everyday items and 'just in case' utility clutter. Letting go of things like old hobby supplies and redundant kitchen gadgets proved way more emotionally draining than I ever anticipated. I am curious to hear from others who have walked this path: what specific type of everyday clutter was the absolute hardest for you to finally part with?

Replies (8)

TechnoTitan
Replied: 17.02.2025 08:02
For me, it was definitely books. I used to think my bookshelves defined my identity. Parting with novels I'd never read again felt like losing a piece of myself, but once I finally donated them, the mental weight lifted was incredible.
VortexVagabond
Replied: 27.02.2025 04:24
Kitchen gadgets were my Waterloo! I kept holding onto an expensive pasta maker because 'what if I suddenly decide to make homemade fettuccine?' Letting go of the 'potential self' was way harder than letting go of the physical items.

Paper clutter was an absolute nightmare for me. Old journals, school notes from a decade ago, and instruction manuals for appliances I no longer own. Scanning the important bits helped, but it took months to actually toss the physical paper.
